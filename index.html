About Us

Quicksell Guns & Ammo is a licensed FFL dealer and professional gunsmithing shop based in Westport, Indiana. As a licensed SOT holder, we specialize in the sale and transfer of suppressors and other Class 3 NFA items. From expert barrel threading to precision custom work on both new and historic firearms, we handle your hardware with unmatched trade expertise.
